Depending on how you use the website, we may process the following categories of personal data:

  • Profile information that you choose to provide.

Order and delivery information

  • Full name.

We process data where necessary to provide the services you request, including:

  • Processing your order.
  • Processing payment.
  • Arranging delivery.
  • Providing order history and order-related services.
  • Handling requests relating to purchases, returns or refunds.

Legal obligations

We may process and retain information where this is required to comply with applicable tax, accounting, consumer protection or other legal obligations.

Legitimate interests

We may process limited information where necessary for our legitimate interests, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms. These interests may include:

Consent

Where you create an account, Supabase may process information necessary to authenticate and maintain that account, such as your email address, user identifier and authentication/session information.

Information connected with orders, customer accounts and order history may also be stored through our Supabase database infrastructure where required to provide those services.

Supabase acts as a technology service provider and processes data in accordance with its applicable contractual and data protection obligations.

6. Payments and Stripe

Online card payments are processed using Stripe Checkout.

When you proceed to payment, certain personal and transaction information necessary to complete and secure the payment is processed by Stripe.

Depending on the transaction, this may include information such as your name, email address, billing or shipping information, transaction amount and payment method information.

Stripe may process personal data for purposes including payment processing, fraud prevention, security and compliance with financial and regulatory obligations.

Your use of Stripe's payment services is also subject to Stripe's own privacy practices.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

This section forms our Cookie and Local Storage Policy. We do not maintain a separate Cookie Policy page.

Necessary storage

The website may use cookies, local storage, session storage or similar browser technologies where they are necessary for functions requested by the user or for the proper operation of the website.

These technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Maintaining authentication and account sessions.
  • Keeping products in your shopping cart.
  • Maintaining your wishlist.
  • Preserving website functionality or user selections.
  • Supporting security and preventing misuse.

Where storage or access to information on your device is strictly necessary to provide a service that you have requested, consent is not required.

Optional cookies

Non-essential cookies or similar technologies, such as analytics, advertising or other optional tracking technologies, will not be used without obtaining any consent required by law.

You can choose “Accept all” or “Necessary only” in the cookie notice and change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Your choice is stored locally in this browser. No optional analytics or advertising trackers are currently active.

If optional cookies or similar technologies are introduced in the future, users will be provided with appropriate information and controls before those technologies are activated where consent is required.

Browser controls

You can also remove cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Removing necessary browser data may cause features such as your account session, cart or wishlist to be reset or stop functioning correctly.
