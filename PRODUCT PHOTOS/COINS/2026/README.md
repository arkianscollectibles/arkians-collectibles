# Original 2026 coin photographs

All 16 JPEGs are unchanged copies from the owner-provided `2026.zip`. The filenames describe the photographed coins; all years are 2026 as specified by the owner. No recolouring, retouching, sharpening or resampling was performed.

`manifest.json` records each source filename, SHA-256, source dimensions, measured coin bounds and display offsets. The 2026 gallery in `coins.html` scales the images uniformly and centres the measured coin outlines to the same maximum diameter as the existing catalogue reference. The source files remain unchanged; titles appear outside the photographs.

These entries are a photo gallery. They do not introduce prices or checkout product IDs. Official-name verification for 2026 remains pending; see `coin-name-verification.json` at the repository root. Existing product names for earlier years were checked separately against ECB descriptions; their prices and IDs were preserved.
